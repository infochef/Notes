# Artificial Intelligence (AI) — Simulating human intelligence

├──► **Types of AI** 
│   ├── Narrow AI (Weak AI) — AI designed for a specific task (e.g., Siri, Google Assistant, ChatGPT)
│   ├── General AI (AGI) — AI with human-like cognitive abilities (not yet achieved)
│   ├── Superintelligent AI — AI surpassing human intelligence (theoretical)
│   ├── **Agentic AI** — AI with autonomy to make decisions and perform actions (e.g., autonomous vehicles, robots)
│   │   ├── **Current Development**: Increasing use in robotics, self-driving cars (e.g., Tesla, Waymo)
│   │   ├── **Research Focus**: Multi-agent collaboration, agent memory, adaptive autonomy
│   │   └── **Related Phase**: Emerging at the **Limited Memory** and progressing toward **Theory of Mind**
│   └── Conversational AI — Specialized in human-computer interaction (e.g., chatbots, virtual assistants)
│       └── **Current Development**: Advanced NLP models like GPT-4, conversational bots for customer service
│
├──► **Phases of AI**
│   ├── **Reactive Machines** — Basic AI systems that respond to specific tasks without memory (e.g., IBM’s Deep Blue)
│   ├── **Limited Memory** — AI with short-term memory (e.g., self-driving cars, predictive analytics)
│   │   └── **Current Development**: Improved short-term decision-making and pattern recognition
│   │       └── **Used by**: Agentic AI, Reinforcement Learning systems
│   ├── **Theory of Mind** — AI that understands emotions and social context (still in development)
│   │   └── **Future of AI**: Human-like interaction and empathy in machines (emotional AI, robotics)
│   │       └── **Target of**: Agentic AI and next-gen NLP systems
│   └── **Self-Aware AI** — AI that has consciousness and self-understanding (theoretical, future phase)
│       └── **Future of AI**: Development of sentient machines, philosophical and ethical challenges
│
├──► **Mathematics for AI/ML**
│   ├── **Linear Algebra** — Vectors, matrices, matrix ops
│   │   └── Used in: Neural networks, computer vision, PCA
│   ├── **Calculus** — Derivatives, gradients, chain rule
│   │   └── Used in: Backpropagation, optimization
│   ├── **Probability & Statistics**
│   │   └── Used in: Bayesian models, generative models, NLP
│   ├── **Optimization / Linear Programming**
│   │   └── Used in: Gradient descent, cost minimization
│   └── **Discrete Math & Graph Theory**
│       └── Used in: Graph neural networks, search, logic
│
├──► **Machine Learning (ML)** — Learning from data
│   ├──► **Supervised Learning** — Labeled data
│   │   ├── Regression — Predict continuous values (e.g., House Price Prediction)
│   │   └── Classification — Predict categories (e.g., Email Spam Detection)
│   │       ├── Logistic Regression
│   │       ├── Decision Trees
│   │       ├── Random Forest
│   │       ├── SVM (Support Vector Machine)
│   │       └── Neural Networks (basic)
│   │           └── Example: OCR (digit recognition)
│   ├──► **Unsupervised Learning** — Unlabeled data
│   │   ├── Clustering — Grouping data (e.g., Customer Segmentation)
│   │   │   ├── K-Means
│   │   │   ├── DBSCAN
│   │   │   └── Hierarchical Clustering
│   │   └── Dimensionality Reduction — Feature simplification (e.g., Visualizing high-dimensional data)
│   │       ├── PCA (Principal Component Analysis)
│   │       └── t-SNE (t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding)
│   └──► **Reinforcement Learning** — Learn via feedback/rewards
│       ├── Q-Learning
│       ├── Deep Q Networks (DQN)
│       └── Policy Gradient Methods
│           └── Example: Game-playing AI (e.g., AlphaGo)
│       └── **Current Development**: Robotics, AI in video games, optimizing complex systems
│           └── **Used in**: Agentic AI for decision-making autonomy
│
├──► **Deep Learning (DL)** — Neural Networks with many layers (subset of ML)
│   ├── CNN (Convolutional Neural Networks)
│   │   └── Image recognition, object detection (e.g., Face Recognition in phones)
│   ├── RNN (Recurrent Neural Networks)
│   │   └── Sequence prediction (e.g., Stock forecasting)
│   ├── LSTM / GRU — Handle long-term dependencies (e.g., Time series, speech recognition)
│   ├── GANs (Generative Adversarial Networks)
│   │   └── Image/video generation (e.g., DeepFakes)
│   └── Transformers — Process sequences in parallel (powering NLP models)
│       └── Example: GPT, BERT (foundation of modern language models)
│       └── **Current Development**: Transformer-based models leading AI advancements (e.g., GPT-4, BERT)
│
├──► **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — Language understanding & generation
│   ├── Traditional NLP
│   │   ├── Bag of Words
│   │   ├── TF-IDF
│   │   └── Rule-based Parsing
│   │       └── Example: Chatbots with rule-based replies
│   └── Deep Learning-based NLP
│       ├── Word Embeddings — Vector representation of words
│       │   ├── Word2Vec
│       │   ├── GloVe
│       │   └── FastText
│       ├── RNNs / LSTMs — Sequence models
│       ├── Seq2Seq Models — Input-to-output mapping (e.g., Language Translation)
│       ├── Attention Mechanism — Improves focus on relevant parts
│       └── Transformers — Foundation of large models
│           ├── BERT — Bidirectional encoder (e.g., Google Search context understanding)
│           ├── GPT — Generative (e.g., ChatGPT by OpenAI)
│           ├── T5 — Text-to-text transformer
│           ├── LLaMA — Meta AI’s open-source large language model
│           └── Amazon Q — Amazon's enterprise chatbot assistant
│       └── **Current Development**: Advanced language models, real-time translation, sentiment analysis
│       └── **Cross-links**: Prompt Engineering used heavily in NLP interactions
│
├──► **Generative AI** — Creating new data (art, text, audio, etc.)
│   ├── Text Generation (e.g., ChatGPT, GPT-3/4 by OpenAI)
│   ├── Image Generation (e.g., DALL·E by OpenAI, DeepArt)
│   ├── Video & Audio Synthesis (e.g., DeepFake, OpenAI Jukedeck)
│   ├── Music Composition (e.g., Jukedeck, OpenAI MuseNet)
│   ├── **Prompt Engineering** — Crafting inputs to control AI behavior
│   │   ├── Types of Prompting:
│   │   │   ├── Zero-shot Prompting — No examples provided
│   │   │   ├── Few-shot Prompting — Providing limited examples
│   │   │   ├── Chain-of-Thought Prompting — Step-by-step reasoning
│   │   │   ├── System Prompting — Set context for model behavior
│   │   │   └── Role-based Prompting — Identity-guided interaction
│   │   ├── Applications:
│   │   │   ├── Chatbots & Assistants (NLP-based)
│   │   │   ├── Creative Content Generation (text, visuals, code)
│   │   │   ├── Enterprise AI (e.g., automated documentation)
│   │   │   └── Prompt Debugging & Evaluation
│   │   └── **Current Development**: Prompt marketplaces, auto-prompting tools, RLHF-based fine-tuning
│   └── Example: **ChatGPT**, **DALL·E**
│       └── **Current Development**: Image, video, and music generation reaching new levels of creativity (e.g., DeepFakes, text-to-image synthesis)
│
├──► **Predictive AI** — Forecasting future outcomes
│   ├── Predictive Analytics — Forecast based on historical data (e.g., Sales forecasting)
│   ├── Time Series Forecasting — Predict future trends (e.g., Stock price prediction, weather)
│   ├── Customer Behavior Prediction — Targeting and retention models (e.g., Amazon, Netflix)
│   └── Example: **Amazon's Recommender System**, **Financial Stock Prediction**
│       └── **Current Development**: Advanced recommendation systems, predictive modeling in finance and healthcare
│
├──► **AI in Medical Science**
│   ├── **Neural Networks in Healthcare** — Analyzing medical images (e.g., X-rays, MRIs), diagnosing diseases
│   │   ├── CNNs for image classification (e.g., detecting cancerous cells in radiology images)
│   │   ├── RNNs for sequential data analysis (e.g., patient health records over time)
│   │   ├── Transfer Learning — Fine-tuning pre-trained models for medical tasks (e.g., disease detection)
│   │   └── **Current Development**: AI assisting radiologists, predicting patient outcomes, drug discovery (e.g., IBM Watson Health, DeepMind’s protein folding)
│   ├── **Predictive Models in Medicine** — Forecasting disease outbreaks, predicting patient risk factors
│   │   ├── Predictive Diagnostics (e.g., predicting heart disease risk)
│   │   └── Personalized Medicine — Tailoring treatments to individual genetic profiles
│   ├── **AI for Drug Discovery & Development** — Speeding up the development of new medications
│   │   ├── AI-driven drug design (e.g., DeepMind, Insilico Medicine)
│   │   ├── Accelerating clinical trials through predictive models
│   └── **Future of AI in Medicine**: Full integration in patient care, AI-powered diagnostics, robotic surgery, and precision medicine
│
├──► **Quantum Programming & AI Integration**
│   ├── What is Quantum Programming?
│   │   ├── Programming based on quantum mechanics principles (qubits, superposition, entanglement)
│   │   ├── Qubits can represent 0 and 1 at the same time (unlike classical bits)
│   │   ├── Enables exponential computation power for specific problems (vs. classical computing)
│   │   └── Programs are written to control quantum gates and circuits
│   ├── Quantum Machine Learning (QML) — Leveraging quantum computing for complex pattern recognition
│   │   ├── Use Cases:
│   │   │   ├── Drug discovery acceleration
│   │   │   ├── Optimization problems in finance, logistics, supply chains
│   │   │   └── Quantum-enhanced neural networks and kernel methods (experimental)
│   ├── Quantum AI Frameworks:
│   │   ├── Qiskit (IBM) — Python-based quantum programming toolkit
│   │   ├── PennyLane (Xanadu) — Hybrid quantum/classical machine learning
│   │   └── TensorFlow Quantum (Google) — Quantum extensions for deep learning
│   └── **Current Development**: 
│       ├── Primarily in academic and industrial R&D
│       ├── Hybrid classical–quantum AI systems under experimentation
│       └── Still early-stage, but promising for future breakthroughs in cryptography, AI, material science, and simulation
